Link imported Garmin Activities to Sessions: the app suggests the Session planned on the Activity's local day, to confirm or decline, and any Session of the selected Plan can be picked by hand. Linking records the Activity's distance and duration on the Session; unlinking gives the Session back what had been logged on it by hand, or leaves it not done. Unplanned Activities show in the calendar on their day and count in the dashboard totals, without counting a linked Activity twice.
